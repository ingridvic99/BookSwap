BookSwap How it works
📚 Zero money. Pure stories.

Books go around. Money stays in your wallet.

Biblioswap is a community where readers trade books they have already loved for books they are dying to read. No money changes hands, just stories, ideas, and a little trust between book lovers. Every trade means one less book abandoned on a shelf and one more reader discovering something wonderful. The only thing you might spend is a little postage, the rest is pure literary joy. teste

How a swap works

Five steps between you and a book you've been wanting to read.

  1. Find a book

    Browse thousands of books offered by readers near you and around the world.

  2. Propose a swap

    Offer one of your books in exchange. Write a short message to break the ice.

  3. Agree together

    Chat directly with the other reader. No platform interference.

  4. Ship your book

    Send by post or arrange local pick-up. Whatever works for both of you.

  5. Enjoy & review

    Your new book arrives. Rate the swap and keep the cycle going.
